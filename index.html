THE DESIGN / 03

Technology that
stays out of the way.

Our hardware goals are focused on comfort, clarity and responsible use.

01 / OPTICS◉

Discreet display.

A transparent near-eye display designed to minimize external light leakage. Optical performance is yet to be validated.

02 / CONTROL✧

Natural controls.

Voice, smartphone controls and experimental hand gestures. Choose what feels right for you.

03 / PRIVACY▣

You're in control.

On-demand visual assistance, opt-in conversation capture and planned hardware activity indicators.

DESIGNED FOR THE EVERYDAY

Look up.
Stay present.

From a quick translation to a question about what you see, Aira is designed for short, helpful interactions rather than another screen to stare at.
